Explore the basics

Make something. See what happens.

Sketch a cup to catch a falling dot. Build a stack of shapes. Slice a drawing into pieces, send them swirling, then undo and try another idea. There are no scores or levels to finish. The page is yours to explore.

Follow your hand

Use your finger or Apple Pencil. Choose a fine line, a bold mark, a solid fill or an open outline. Pick from 72 colors, mix a custom color, or let Rainbow choose a new color for each stroke.

Come back to a good idea

Your canvases save automatically to a local gallery. Start a fresh page whenever you like, or reopen an earlier one and keep playing. Save a picture of the paper and ink to Photos.
